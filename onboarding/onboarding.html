Welcome to AI Quiz Solve

Instant answers on any quiz platform.

Activate

Press Ctrl+Shift+U or click the extension icon to toggle on. The badge shows ON when active.

Double-click a question

Double-click any quiz question to auto-detect and solve it. Hold Shift + double-click to get a detailed explanation instead.

Snap It — Screenshot Solve

Press Ctrl+Shift+S or right-click → Solve with Screenshot to capture any area of the screen. Works on images, PDFs, Canvas, and tricky layouts. When text detection fails, Screenshot Solve kicks in automatically.

Choose your answer mode

Auto — selects the correct answer for you
Highlight — subtly highlights the right option
Clipboard — copies the answer silently

Notifications are on by default so you can see progress. Turn them off anytime in Settings.
Screenshot Solve is enabled by default. It also activates automatically when text detection can't find the answer choices.
Answers default to English. Change the language anytime in Settings → Answer Language.
We only read quiz content when activated. Your browsing data is never collected.
