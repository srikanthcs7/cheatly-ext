🎓 AI Quiz Solve is a study assistant that helps you understand and answer quiz questions instantly. Double-click any question to get the answer, or hold Shift for a detailed explanation. Works on Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, Google Forms, Brightspace (D2L), Schoology, and 20+ other quiz platforms. No sign-up required for the free tier.

⚡ HOW IT WORKS

1️⃣ Install the extension (no sign-up required for free tier)
2️⃣ Press Ctrl+Shift+U (Cmd+Shift+U on Mac) to activate on any page
3️⃣ Double-click a question to get an instant answer
4️⃣ Shift + Double-click a question for a detailed explanation
5️⃣ Use Screenshot Solve (Ctrl+Shift+S) for image-based or tricky questions

🎯 THREE ANSWER MODES

Choose how answers are delivered:
🟢 Auto-Answer: automatically selects or fills the correct answer
🟡 Highlight: subtly highlights the correct option on screen
📋 Clipboard: silently copies the answer for you to paste

📸 SCREENSHOT SOLVE

Press Ctrl+Shift+S to capture any region of the screen. Screenshot Solve reads images, diagrams, charts, PDFs, and complex layouts that text detection cannot handle. It also activates automatically when the extension detects that answer choices are embedded in images.

🎨 DRAW-TO-SOLVE

Solve visual and handwritten problems by drawing directly on the screen.

👁️ VISION SUPPORT

AI Quiz Solve automatically detects images, diagrams, graphs, and code snippets within questions and sends them to the AI for more accurate answers. No configuration needed.
